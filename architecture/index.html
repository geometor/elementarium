Rational Brewster Spread Identity¶

Under Rational Trigonometry, Brewster’s law \(\tan\theta_B = n\) avoids transcendental functions entirely. The exact incidence spread \(s_B = \sin^2\theta_B\) for fused silica (\(n_D = \frac{2917}{2000}\)) is strictly rational:

\[s_B = \frac{n_D^2}{1 + n_D^2} = \frac{8\,508\,889}{12\,508\,889} \approx 0.6802274\]

Inclining two flat fused-silica plates at spread \(s_B\) with orthogonal planes of incidence creates an all-glass, degradation-free cross-polarizer analyzer capable of reading T2 birefringent strata indefinitely.
