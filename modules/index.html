Modules¶

geometor.elementarium follows the GEOMETOR Mixin Architecture, separating optical tier physics, Mark I bench geometry, and Primer reticle export into composable modules:

  • geometor.elementarium — Public package interface exporting Elementarium, TIERS, DEFAULT_MARK_I_BENCH, PRIMER_PLATES, build_keystone_plate_model(), and export_plate_svg().

  • geometor.elementarium.tiers — OpticalTier specifications, exact fused-silica Brewster spread (brewster_spread()), and TiersMixin.

  • geometor.elementarium.bench — MarkIBenchSpec golden-station rail geometry, exact float-free geometor.model Keystone builder (build_keystone_plate_model()), and BenchMixin.

  • geometor.elementarium.plates — PrimerPlate sequence (P0–P5), 1:1 50 mm × 50 mm SVG reticle serializer (render_keystone_svg()), and PlatesMixin.

  • geometor.elementarium.cli — Rich command-line interface (status, tiers, plates, export-masks).
