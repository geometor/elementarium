**Elementarium • Zero-context optical bootstrap & 100,000-year geometric archive**

## Mission

`geometor/elementarium` is part of the interconnected [GEOMETOR](https://geometor.com) semantic digital archive. It designs, prototypes, and formalizes a monolithic, unpowered optical archive in fused silica ($\text{SiO}_2$) engineered for a 100,000-year deep-time preservation horizon. By encoding foundational constructive geometry (*Euclid I.1* Vesica Piscis, Rational Trigonometry, and invariant proportion) as a zero-context primer before natural language, it liberates human knowledge from electrical power grids and fragile digital infrastructure into permanent optical geometry.
