====================
Optical Architecture
====================

To guarantee readability across :math:`10^5` years without surviving electronics or linguistic continuity, the Elementarium stratifies information across four passive optical regimes in fused silica (:math:`\text{SiO}_2`).

-----------------------

   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 8 24 28 40

   * - Tier
     - Name
     - Illumination Condition
     - Epistemic & Optical Function
   * - ``T0``

   * - ``T1``

     - Performs passive optical nomography and exposes self-describing symbolic DAG frames (``G.1``–``G.601``) for machine execution.

Rational Brewster Spread Identity
---------------------------------

Under Rational Trigonometry, Brewster's law :math:`\tan\theta_B = n` avoids transcendental functions entirely. The exact incidence spread :math:`s_B = \sin^2\theta_B` for fused silica (:math:`n_D = \frac{2917}{2000}`) is strictly rational:

.. math::

   s_B = \frac{n_D^2}{1 + n_D^2} = \frac{8\,508\,889}{12\,508\,889} \approx 0.6802274

Inclining two flat fused-silica plates at spread :math:`s_B` with orthogonal planes of incidence creates an all-glass, degradation-free cross-polarizer analyzer capable of reading ``T2`` birefringent strata indefinitely.
