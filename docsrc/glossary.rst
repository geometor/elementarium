========
Glossary
========

.. glossary::
   :sorted:

   Brewster Spread
      Exact Rational Trigonometry spread :math:`s_B = \sin^2\theta_B = \frac{n^2}{1 + n^2}` at which p-polarized light transmits without reflection across a dielectric interface, yielding 100% s-polarized reflected light.

   Dead-Reader Paradox
      Failure mode of high-density archival media in which decoding the initial physical symbols requires an industrial and software ecosystem more fragile than the medium itself.

   Form Birefringence
      Sub-wavelength periodic nanostructuring inside isotropic fused silica (:math:`\text{SiO}_2`) that induces controllable optical retardance and slow-axis azimuth orientation with geological thermal stability.

   Vesica Keystone
      ``Plate 0`` of the Elementarium Primer, deriving unit length, orthogonality, equilateral spread (:math:`3/4`), and golden-section rail spacing (:math:`\phi`) from two equal intersecting circles (*Euclid I.1*).
