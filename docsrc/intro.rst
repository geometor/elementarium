============
Elementarium
============

*Passive optical archive and zero-context geometric bootstrap architecture for 100,000-year knowledge preservation.*

Contemporary information systems suffer from an acute **Dead-Reader Paradox**: the denser our magnetic and solid-state storage becomes, the shorter its physical survival and the steeper the industrial chain required to read its first bit. Deprived of active electrical power, semiconductor fabs, character encodings, and living natural language, cloud and solid-state archives decay into unreadable noise within decades.

**Elementarium** addresses deep-time preservation across a :math:`10^5`-year horizon by encoding foundational deductive, metrological, scientific, and computational knowledge directly into passive fused silica (:math:`\text{SiO}_2`). Nothing is required to initiate readout except **a beam of light and a projection surface**.

Core Engineering Principles
---------------------------

1. **Concentric Optical Escalation**: A monolithic or modular stack of fused-silica plates encodes four progressive optical regimes—from raw uncollimated shadow projection (``T0``), to collimated lithographic reticles (``T1``), to crossed Brewster-angle birefringence (``T2``), to combinatorial Vernier and cold-boot symbolic frames (``T3``). Each stratum visually instructs the observer how to align or construct the optical instrument required to read the next.
2. **Constructive Geometry Before Language**: Rather than opening with arbitrary alphabets or isolated prime sequences, the Elementarium establishes equivalence, implication, invariance, ratio, and proof directly from *Euclid I.1* (the Vesica Piscis), Rational Trigonometry (:math:`Q`, :math:`s`), and golden-section proportion (:math:`\phi`).
3. **Substrate-Anchored Metrology**: Fused quartz (:math:`\text{SiO}_2`) acts simultaneously as archival medium, optical element, and physical standard—anchoring unit length, refractive dispersion (:math:`n_D = \frac{2917}{2000}`), and exact Brewster polarization spread (:math:`s_B = \frac{n_D^2}{1 + n_D^2}`) in immutable mineral physics.
4. **Mark I Desktop Prototype**: Phase 1 brings the architecture onto the optical bench as a compact, unpowered ``50 mm × 50 mm`` fused-silica reticle assembly verifying end-to-end human and camera cold-boot recovery.
