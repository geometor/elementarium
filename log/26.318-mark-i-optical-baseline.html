Inaugural Optical Specification & Mark I Desktop Bench Baseline¶

Initialized geometor.elementarium (v0.1.0) to establish the physical, optical, and constructive-geometric architecture for a \(10^5\)-year passive fused-silica archive, anchored immediately on building the Mark I Desktop Optical Prototype.

Engineering Milestones¶

  • Four-Tier Passive Optical Stack (geometor.elementarium.tiers): Defined concentric readout escalation across raw aperture shadow projection (T0), collimated chrome-on-quartz reticles (T1), crossed Brewster-angle birefringence (T2), and combinatorial/fiduciary cold-boot frames (T3). Formulated Brewster’s angle in exact Rational Trigonometry via \(s_B = \frac{n_D^2}{1 + n_D^2} = \frac{8\,508\,889}{12\,508\,889}\) for fused silica (\(n_D = \frac{2917}{2000}\)).

  • Mark I Benchtop Rail & Keystone Mask (geometor.elementarium.bench, geometor.elementarium.plates): Constructed Plate 0 (Vesica Keystone) in geometor.model with zero sp.Float approximations, deriving the self-verifying golden axial stations (\(u, u\phi, u\phi^2, u\phi^3\)) and exporting a 1:1 50 mm × 50 mm SVG reticle mask.
