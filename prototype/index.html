Mark I Desktop Prototype¶

The immediate engineering objective of geometor.elementarium is the Mark I Desktop Optical Prototype: a benchtop, completely unpowered passive optical instrument built around 50 mm × 50 mm fused-silica plates on a golden-proportioned kinematic rail.

Benchtop Geometry & Station Detents¶

All axial station positions derive from a single physical unit chord \(u = |AB| = 20\text{ mm}\) established directly on Plate 0:

  • Entrance Aperture (`Station 0`): \(0\text{ mm}\) — Plate 0 (Vesica Keystone) receiving external sunlight, LED point illumination, or heliostat input.

  • Collimator Stage (`Station 1`): \(u \cdot \phi = 10(1 + \sqrt{5})\text{ mm} \approx 32.361\text{ mm}\) — Secondary pinhole slit / fused-silica planoconvex singlet (\(f \approx 32.4\text{ mm}\)).

  • Reticle Stage (`Station 2`): \(u \cdot \phi^2 = 10(3 + \sqrt{5})\text{ mm} \approx 52.361\text{ mm}\) — 50 mm × 50 mm chrome-on-quartz and birefringent waveplate carrier.

  • Brewster Analyzer Stage (`Station 3`): \(u \cdot \phi^3 = 20(2 + \sqrt{5})\text{ mm} \approx 84.721\text{ mm}\) — Removable / rotatable fused-silica flat inclined at \(\theta_B = \arctan(1.4585) \approx 55.564^\circ\).

Notice the additive self-verification built into the rail:

\[u + u\phi = u\phi^2, \qquad u\phi + u\phi^2 = u\phi^3\]

Any observer holding Plate 0 can verify every axial spacing on the optical bench by direct compass transfer without a millimeter scale.

Candidate Benchtop Fabrication Paths¶

  1. T0 Aperture Keystone: Laser-cut brass / blackened stainless steel foil or etched silicon aperture mask mounted in a 50 mm × 50 mm quartz frame.

  2. T1 Primer Reticles: Photolithographic chrome-on-quartz reticles (1-2 um minimum feature size) or UV-laser-ablated metallic thin-film masks on fused silica.

  3. T2 Birefringent Demonstrator: - Benchtop Iteration A: Patterned multi-order quartz / polymer retarder sectors laminated between fused-silica flats, completely transparent in unpolarized T1 projection and high-contrast between crossed \(55.56^\circ\) glass Brewster plates. - Benchtop Iteration B: Femtosecond-laser-written form-birefringent nanogratings inside monolithic fused silica.

  4. T3 Combinatorial & Sensor Frame: Dual counter-rotating Vernier quartz reticles paired with a high-density hexagonal fiduciary matrix encoding the minimal DAG evaluator.
